The entry is a single-line joke about office life: the daily standup meeting has finally been given chairs, which is fitting, because nobody in the meeting has stood up for anything in years. The setup is a detail anyone who has worked in an office recognises instantly — the ritual standup that long ago stopped being about standing — and the punchline turns on the double meaning of "standing up": nobody rises from their seat, and nobody takes a stand either. It reads as one tight sentence, needs no explanation, and lands on first read.
